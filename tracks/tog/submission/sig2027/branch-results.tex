\subsection{Keeping the edited future}
A disposable preview omits material outside its query. To keep the edit, the system must produce the full state at the crossing, finish that step's native tail and advance playback. We issued the keep or discard command only after receiving the same unresolved preview handle. Ordinary replay resumes its interrupted scan; the regional solver reconciles pending material. Both matched the full native state on the event frame and the following frame for all four development edits.
Across 80 calls (64 measured), slice minus ordinary median delivered time on discard was $-36$, $-125$, $-184$ and $-166$ ms in woodpile A/B, woodpile B/A, trunk A/B and trunk B/A order. On keep and playback the differences were $-7$, $+33$, $+3$ and $-27$ ms. The pending state required 0.154--0.465 MB in additional typed storage. At the point estimates the reverse woodpile edit stopped favoring the slice if at least 79\% of queries were kept. Individual timing waves yield different crossover points and in some cases none. No creator keep frequency was measured.
