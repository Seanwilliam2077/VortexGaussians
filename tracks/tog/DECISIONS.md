# Decisions after the ordinary-control screen

Written before T03 results on 27 October 2026. T02 is one researcher-authored development target. A result in this small periodic passive-smoke model is neither an external artist requirement nor a TOG contribution.

The first question is whether this particular target has a *demonstrated joint solution* and whether ignoring protection actually breaks an approved region. Use the saved full-frame acceptance metrics, not an averaged objective or the qualification run's deliberately nonzero multipliers.

| T03 observation | Supported conclusion | Next informative work |
|---|---|---|
| Edit-only meets editing but breaks protection; joint meets every gate | One numerical instance with a feasible solution and a protection conflict | Identify where the joint solve pays additional work. Then obtain a coherent sequence of independent creative edits before evaluating reuse, including construction and refresh costs. No speedup claim from the two different stopping criteria. |
| Both meet editing and protection | This target does not expose a protection conflict under these ordinary methods | Do not build a response cache for it. Move task selection toward a concrete creative requirement with spatial/temporal preservation that matters to its author. Preserve the easy case as a baseline. |
| Edit-only meets editing and breaks protection; joint fails | A conflict is observed, but joint feasibility is unestablished within this budget | Inspect saved residuals, returned controls and constraint trajectories. Do not declare the task infeasible or a new solver necessary. Any further feasibility attempt needs a specific diagnosis and a separately declared finite ordinary-method comparison. |
| Both fail editing | Neither arm establishes the requested task | Inspect whether failures concentrate near the authored image seam, early exact frames, or control limits. Do not relax the target or gates to relabel this run. Decide whether the image task expresses a useful physical operation before spending more simulation. |
| Joint succeeds while edit-only fails editing | Different nonlinear optimization paths reached different outcomes | Record the result without calling it protection conflict resolution or acceleration; compare the actual saved states before choosing a mechanism. |
| Gradient qualification, execution or numerical validity fails | The screen cannot answer its research question | Preserve the failure. Repair a concrete implementation error if present; a changed numerical protocol must be new and must not overwrite this one. |

## Evidence needed beyond a qualified instance

1. A specific creative operation and credible reason that both the edit and the preserved content matter. An authored warp supplies an input, not that evidence.
2. A limitation or substantial cost in a competitive ordinary workflow, under the same quality requirements and control freedom.
3. A mechanism that addresses that limitation and differs substantively from existing control, reduced-order and reuse methods. Conventional components may be useful, but their assembly is not automatically novel.
4. Repeated, independent scenes/edits and a fair total-cost or capability comparison, with failures and required setup included.

The next action should close the earliest unresolved item. More interfaces, audit counts or easy toy variants do not move later items forward by themselves. The [art track](../art/ART_BRIEF.md) can provide concrete creative questions through actual practice, but it is not required to adopt the TOG model and its audience or author evidence must not be fabricated.
