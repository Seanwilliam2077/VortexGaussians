## Implication for this project

The distinction that matters is between a field being divergence-free, a reduced continuous system being Hamiltonian, a material map preserving Casimirs, and a discrete solver retaining physical moments or energy. These are not interchangeable. T76's moment drift cannot by itself justify a new Hamiltonian method, and ordinary moment-constrained projection remains a necessary allowed alternative.

The screen adds specific competing capabilities and an evaluation limitation to our research map. It does **not** establish that the aspect-dependent restriction is a practical bottleneck for our intended scenes, that every ordinary evaluator has it, or that a proposed replacement beats direct quadrature, tail-aware FMM, refinement or a velocity representation. It also does not supply a globally admissible nonlinear material carrier with cheap self-induced evaluation.

Therefore “deforming Gaussian vortices,” “Hamiltonian compact state,” “use FMM,” and “avoid a mandatory grid” are insufficient standalone method claims. No new candidate is selected simply because the six-source screen found no matching complete solution. [DECISION.md](DECISION.md) fixes the next evidence boundary. The full SIGGRAPH objective remains active and unmet; the historical TOG score is not changed.
