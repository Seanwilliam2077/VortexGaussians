# T56 — ordinary conservative coupled evolution

Use T55's convention omega_theta=r*q=partial_z u_r-partial_r u_z, psi=r^2 phi, u_r=-psi_z/r, u_z=psi_r/r, and -(phi_rr+3phi_r/r+phi_zz)=q. For inviscid no-swirl Euler, q is advected and partial_r(r*u_r)+partial_z(r*u_z)=0. Thus q is a conservative scalar under annular measure r dr dz; equivalently w=rq obeys w_t+partial_r(u_r*w)+partial_z(u_z*w)=0. This is a five-dimensional elliptic interpretation, not five-dimensional incompressible transport. No new source search is used; the mathematical ancestry and sign limitations remain in [T55](../t55-axisymmetric-reference/SOURCES.md).

## Stored field and initialization

On uniform cells [r-,r+]x[z-,z+], V=((r+^2-r-^2)/2)*h and qbar=(integral r*q dr dz)/V. The radial reconstruction centroid is rc=(2/3)*(r+^3-r-^3)/(r+^2-r-^2), which differs from the arithmetic midpoint rm by h^2/(12rm). Exact Gaussian annular initialization uses separated radial exponential differences and axial erf/erfc differences, with q=1.5 exp(-(r^2+z^2)/2). Positive-tail erfc differences and expm1 avoid unnecessary far-tail cancellation. No clipping is used.

For transport and metrics, reconstruct qbar+sr*(r-rc)+sz*(z-zc). Minmod slopes use neighboring radial-centroid differences and uniform axial differences. Axis ghost q is reflected; exterior axial/radial ghost averages are zero, implementing zero inflow with interior upwind outflow. Multiply both slopes by theta=min(1,(1-64*machine_epsilon)*qbar/dev), where dev=abs(sr)*max(rc-r-,r+-rc)+abs(sz)*h/2 and theta=1 for dev=0. This preserves the r-weighted average, bounds every reconstructed cell value by [0,2qbar] in exact arithmetic and limits negative multidimensional corners. It modifies slopes, not stored q or measured output by clipping. The polynomial represented for diagnostics and comparisons is explicitly this reconstruction; the elliptic source below is its piecewise-constant cell-average approximation.

## Current-source reconstruction and flux

Reuse the T55 FV Poisson matrix and solver unchanged, now with qbar as its source. The piecewise-constant source field has exact five-dimensional radial mass qbar*h*W_i, W_i=(r+^4-r-^4)/4. A boundary matrix multiplies that mass by the 5D angular kernel at source cell midpoints, with 32 or 64 fixed angular Gauss nodes. Exact W_i does not make the whole source-cell kernel integral exact. Boundary targets remain on faces, source points strictly inside; no diagonal self term is dropped. Continuous near-target singularity and truncation remain approximation issues measured by the controls. This matrix is geometric only; its multiplication uses the current stage qbar every time.

Interpolate phi to interior grid vertices by averaging four adjacent cell values. At outer vertices average the two neighboring Dirichlet face values, with separately integrated corner values; set psi=0 on the axis. Multiply nodal phi by r_vertex^2. Integrated face volume fluxes are F_r=-difference_z(psi) and F_z=difference_r(psi). The sum of outward face fluxes cancels algebraically in each cell; the axis radial flux is exactly zero. The q flux at a face is F times the upwind reconstructed face value. A shared face flux enters two neighbors with opposite signs, ensuring the circulation ledger.

For a forward Euler stage, qbar_new=qbar-dt*sum(outward q_flux)/V. If reconstructed donor faces lie in [0,2qbar] and dt*sum(max(outward F,0))/V<=1/2, outgoing removal cannot exceed qbar and incoming flux is nonnegative. The implemented stage checks enforce this sufficient positivity condition. SSPRK2 forms q1=q+dt*L(q), then qnew=.5*q+.5*(q1+dt*L(q1)), using a newly reconstructed velocity at q1. Its circulation change is minus dt/2 times the sum of the two outward boundary q fluxes. No stage reuses analytic initial boundary values or changes dt after a failed CFL check.

Gamma=integral r*q is circulation in the meridional plane. It differs from axial impulse I_z=pi*integral r^3*q and from the invariant volume integral of q^2. In the unbounded inviscid equation integral r*q^2 is transported without change; physical enstrophy integral r^3*q^2 can change through stretching. Finite boundary flux and numerical drift must be kept separate. Full-box moments of the reconstructed field and outer two-cell fractions are empirical support diagnostics, not proven exterior bounds.

## Saved-field comparison

Nested grids share the fixed core boundary. For each common fine cell choose its containing cell in each source grid, evaluate each source's already specified polynomial at 3x2 Gauss points, and integrate 2*pi*r^3*(qA-qB)^2. This retains subcell differences; no averaging or alignment is used to hide them. Radial degree is at most five and axial degree at most two, so this rule is exact for the represented squared difference in real arithmetic. Floating-point calculations and the underlying solution remain uncertified. The denominator uses the control/finer member over the same core.

Use the same quadrature for full-box moments and diagnostics, which are polynomials of no higher degree. The analytic initial meridional covariance is diag((4-pi)/2,1); it supplies one common shape normalization. The initial numerical source reconstruction is checked anew against T55's analytic ordinary initial u/J. Passing the short numerical protocol supplies a necessary reference, not a method benefit or general fluid solver claim.
