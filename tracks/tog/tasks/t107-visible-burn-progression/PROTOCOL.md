# Does a first material event predict substantial burning in the camera view?

For the known original A/B and B/A Firewoods takes, compare three questions through 180 steps from the second click. When does the first cold exterior-shell site ignite? When has one quarter of the eligible sites ignited? When have the ignited sites accrued one quarter of the eligible shell's visible Gaussian opacity contribution in a reference camera? Use the already staged foreground plus the real associated calibrated camera list. Fix the first and middle camera in numeric ID order (index floor(N/2)), and evaluate both native orders without moving any brush. Seed and future event ledgers are historical and their first-shell answers known. This is a development diagnostic. Freeze this rule before calculating the other answers.

Define the camera matte from all foreground splats before the edit, without smoke, emission or background. At pixel centers of the image with calibrated intrinsics scaled to one quarter its original resolution, project each covariance with the linearized perspective Jacobian, add 0.3 pixel-squared to the two diagonal entries, reject means behind the camera, truncate the footprint at squared Mahalanobis distance 9, clamp per-splat sigmoid-opacity times Gaussian to 0.99 and drop alpha below 1/255. Sort all splats by increasing center depth, original ID breaking ties. Assign a splat its sum of front-to-back transmittance times its alpha across pixels, without early termination. This declared fixed-view footprint is a proxy for how much material the camera sees; it does not measure the visible flame or account for intervening scene geometry outside the foreground. First/quarter site count and quarter matte can disagree legitimately. If the eligible subset has zero mass the matte question is undefined. Do not adjust the fraction if the deadline censors a result.

Preserve native frame-by-frame cumulative mass and counts, reference IDs and weights, original camera descriptors, raster cost and small view summaries. Verify composited mass conservation with total terminal opacity, quaternion orientation and principal axes on synthetic tests, at least a cropped independent pixelwise reduction, monotonicity and fresh-set eligibility. Record costs honestly when comparing native caching: an immutable calibrated view matte could be reused across repeated edits; computing one on the fly for a new view is additional work. A result different from first ignition identifies a distinct output. A new speed claim would need matched exact forward implementations for this output and prospective validation.

Production motivation comes from animator-authored fire progression and camera-visible set selection in the SIGGRAPH 2026 Hoppers production talk. Their hero rig can keyframe a growth signal, an ordinary alternative when timing is authored directly. Their team did not request or test this quarter-matte benchmark.
