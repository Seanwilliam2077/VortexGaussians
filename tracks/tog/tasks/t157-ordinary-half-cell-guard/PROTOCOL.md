The simulator and trajectories are fixed, and no device run is included. [T153 results](../t153-catch-state-transfer/README.md)

For each finite binary16 magnitude word, find the smallest and largest nonnegative binary32 value that stores to it. Test both endpoints, the tie with the next magnitude, both signs, both signed zeros, the subnormal-to-normal transition and the last finite magnitude before overflow. Report how many bins have a unique rounded half and whether this equals half the stored word exactly. The proposed ordinary rule admits zero, every magnitude at least `0x0800`, and even positive magnitude words below `0x0800`. Any disagreement with the full cell calculation refutes the proposed classification. Infinities and NaNs are outside the material contract.
