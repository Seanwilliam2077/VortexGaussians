# How long can an ignition preview reuse an existing take exactly?

24 November 2026. T84 found that the global edit eventually changes 1,266 off-stroke endpoint gains and 12 losses. We now test the prefix on which an editor can return exact material membership by reading the cached A take plus the instantaneous B operation. This is a retrospective, single-shot experiment designed after seeing T84. Its two policy arms are development examples. The program has no access to future edited-state labels. Its result must be saved before comparing to edited trajectories.

Inputs are the original recipe and geometry for source-to-layer mapping, the unedited take's ordered frontier, counters and offer logs, and the edited frontier immediately after the zero-time native B operation. A deployed editor can obtain that last ordering by applying the user's stroke, before any new simulation step. The ordered IDs matter. An A cache may be read through frame 360. Target outputs are active material IDs on frames 181–360 and the horizon through which the native gas and its random stream remain equivalent. Rendered frames, compressed gas and exact post-fork continuation are outside this measurement.

For each policy use native elapsed clocks and ordered first-1,400 graph sources. Verify that pre-existing and direct burners can survive the interval, that the A trace is append-only and that all served graph sources remain the same. The direct B edit preserves the initial gas and cursor. Evolve a shadow frontier by adding A's newly lit sites except those already manually lit. Walk the edited round-robin cursor in native order. At the start of each feed, reconstruct A's layer occupancy from the row's post-feed counts minus its emissions. For matching full predicates, blocked and random-rejected offers leave gas and RNG equivalent even if their source IDs differ. An emitted particle from an equal source uses the same attributes and layer. Stop at the first mismatched fullness predicate or a successful birth with different source IDs. The material scan already completed for that frame. A strict ordinary control stops at the first changed source ID, irrespective of outcome. Record the relative benefit of relaxing the control. If either fails early, report it without adjusting rules. Matched IDs suffice conservatively even where distinct positions might coincide.

Use both policies, at most 180 shadow steps each and no new native simulation. An independent checker compares every asserted material mask to the edited frontier slices, and ordered sources/layers/outcomes for all certified offers, including the partial last frame. Report elapsed archive preparation, trace checking, original cache cost where known, and cumulative T81 timing through the certified frames. A hash guards comparisons, while the equivalence of native gas arrays rests on the source-level induction. Any unsupported state or discrepancy invalidates that claim. The old take is available by assumption and accessing historical full files has a measurable cost. Preserve the actual fork and any limitations on realizing wall-time savings. No claim of broad novelty follows from trace reuse.
