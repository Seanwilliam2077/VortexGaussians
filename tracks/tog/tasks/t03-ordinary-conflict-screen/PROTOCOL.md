## Fixed ordinary solver

This is an explicit fork of the prepared R22 two-mask driver and R21 executed ordinary baseline. Objective and solver numerical settings are unchanged. For each protection ROI/frame, c=mean(error²)/min(.02*referenceRMS,.005)²−1. The 51st constraint is mean(q²)/.2²−1. Edit objective is the mean over 25 per-frame normalized MSEs, with tau=min(.05*targetRMS,.01), plus 1e−4 mean((q/.2)²). PHR contribution is `([max(0,lambda+rho*c)]²−lambda²)/(2*rho)`.

`edit_only` applies mask (0×50,1) to PHR, multiplier updates and the violation used for rho updates. It still records every protection metric. `joint` uses all 51 constraints. Fixed execution order is edit_only then joint; no control, optimizer state or curvature transfers between them. Time differences do not constitute a method speedup because the stop conditions differ.

Each arm allows at most 120 complete rollout attempts, including its zero initial point, all closure/line-search probes and independent accepted-state validation. At most three AL outer iterations, each at most 20 returned L-BFGS steps. Lambda starts at zero, rho at 1; rho grows by 10 up to 100 if active positive violation does not halve. L-BFGS uses lr=1, max_iter=1, max_eval=25, history_size=10, tolerance_grad=1e−9, tolerance_change=1e−12, strong_wolfe. History resets when the AL objective changes. One validation rollout is reserved. If line search reaches the budget, x rolls back to the last independently evaluated accepted state; no trial is labeled accepted. No best-checkpoint selection or restart.

## New-target derivative qualification

Before either optimization, fixed x=.12 times Rademacher(seed 2026102701); two RMS-one Rademacher directions(seed 2026102702); central h=1e−5 and 1e−6. Qualification-only lambda=2 and rho=1 activate every effective PHR branch; record the actual branch margin. The two mask objectives share nine complete forward rollouts and use two backward calls. Every analytic quantity must be finite, all effective branches active, and every `abs(FD−gradDot)/max(abs(gradDot),1e−8)` ≤1e−4. Both masks must pass. Failure stops before optimization, without changing h, seeds, tolerance or target. Qualification cost is reported separately from both 120-rollout budgets and included in the 600-second whole-worker limit.

## Actual acceptance and interpretation

Protected ROIs [35,13,53,33] and [35,33,53,55], edit ROI [10,12,34,55], lower-left half-open pixel coordinates, all 25 frames. Every relevant frame must pass both relative and absolute RMS thresholds; reference RMS ≥.01. Also require all numerical outputs finite, density≥0, alpha in [0,1], coefficient max≤.5, coefficient RMS≤.2 and expanded force max-component≤.5. edit_only stops on edit plus physical/numerical/control gates; joint additionally requires all 50 protection gates. Means of losses are never acceptance tests. The old R22 two-pixel semantic gate is not applied to this different target.

An edit-only accepted result that fits the target but violates protection, plus a joint result passing everything, establishes one ordinary-solver-correctable conflict. If both naturally preserve, no protective bottleneck was exposed. If joint or either arm exhausts its budget, this says the fixed solver did not find a qualifying answer; it does not prove target infeasibility. Transient PHR activation alone does not establish a practically costly conflict, nor a cache method's benefit. Initial zero images should reproduce approval within ordinary recorded numerical differences; their image fit is reported without counting that as a feasible edited solution.
