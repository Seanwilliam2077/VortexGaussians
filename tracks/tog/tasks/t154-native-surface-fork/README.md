# Two external material futures from one guarded gas prefix

The released *Curtain catching* preset lets a stationary sheet accumulate heat before it feeds the surrounding gas. We changed the native catch delay from 0.35 to 0.70 seconds. A conventional fork can keep both actual material atlases beside one evolving gas, determine its split online, and deliver the two edited frame-36 continuations independently. The scene and parameter choice were selected by the investigators. This test establishes the state required for the fork. It contains no human assessment or new speed result. [Registered experiment](PROTOCOL.md) · [Thermal-state source audit](PRELAUNCH_STATE_AUDIT.md)

At the end of frame 8, the scalar guard admitted 12 updates and a full material atlas already differed. The shared execution reproduced each ordinary fork state. Rejected frame 9 would have needed three additional updates. The independently evolved reference arms had 112 and 109 updates by frame 36, then 115 and 112 by frame 37. For the delivered futures through 37 the shared implementation issued 215 full gas updates versus the ordinary pair's 227, adding 12 second-atlas evaluations and 1,040,256 bytes of GPU atlas storage. At the fork, halving the fast progress with float32 arithmetic and storing it as binary16 reproduced all 260,064 slow atlas words. The independent T153 history test already lost its sufficient range criterion at update 8. These measurements keep the cheaper single-atlas comparator open. Both continued branches reproduced the independent arm's coarse and fine scalars, velocity, pressure, floor material, object atlas, integration clocks, step counts, adaptive speed and ember state at frames 36 and 37. No live embers were present. The implementation also carried the two solids' skin/core temperatures and ground or mutual contact clocks, and the ordered radiant sources sampled by the next frame. At frame 8 the branches shared six active radiant sources. At frame 36 the fast arm had 48 sources and the slow arm had 38; the object temperatures had also diverged. These additional fields are outside the original T151 gas checkpoint. Both branches' T151 fields at frame 36 match its independently recorded hashes. Each packet also contains the full released scene and camera, branch parameters, native simulation signature and source revision. Packets use Blackbody's native lossless `.bbc` entry codec. Reopening uses the documented static-fire restore and its thermal supplement. The stock UI's disk-cache discovery has a different directory contract. Local packet paths are `private/default-36.bbc` and `private/slow-36.bbc`. Use the pinned `blackbody.io.simcache.read_entry`, the scene from `settings(arm)`, and the experiment's `prepare(engine, scene)` followed by `restore(engine, scene, 36, entry)`. The restored live engine exposes `Engine.volume_for(scene, 36)` to the native renderer. [Implementation](run.py)

A separate process loaded both local files and restored each branch with a reset engine on a freshly initialized device. The decoded, live frame-36 state and the independently advanced frame-37 state matched each corresponding ordinary receipt in every recorded field. Device and final-engine preparation cost 159.737 s, followed by 6.804 s total to reopen, validate, advance and read both futures. Total host process times were 188.643 s for generation and 167.303 s for the cold continuation. Both completed within their registered budgets.
