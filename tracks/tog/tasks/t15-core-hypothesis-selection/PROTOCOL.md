# T15: three operations, at most one mechanism to qualify

Prepared 28 October 2026 before new literature searches. Implements [T14's next decision](../t14-input-task-audit/NEXT_HYPOTHESIS.md). No new numerical experiment, implementation, training, dependency, large asset or outreach is permitted in this selection task. Old acceptance gates and closed branches remain unchanged.

The three researcher-defined operations are fixed in [CANDIDATES_BEFORE_SEARCH.md](CANDIDATES_BEFORE_SEARCH.md). They are hypotheses, not demonstrated artist needs, new algorithms or established bottlenecks. Their names do not imply novelty. Source reading can reject a card or narrow its mechanism within the same operation; it cannot silently replace it with a fourth candidate or a renamed closed route.

Search budget: two coordinated rounds total, at most three targeted queries per round (one per candidate), and at most six distinct primary paper/author-implementation source families inspected (up to two per candidate). Root coordinates round boundaries; candidate agents may read the selected primary text/code, but may not add searches or a third source without staying inside this global budget. Versions or paper/code pages of the same work count as one family and must be recorded. Existing local research records may eliminate a candidate without using a new source. Search snippets locate sources; only inspected primary material supports a claim. Remaining uncertainty stays unknown after the second round.

For each candidate, record what the closest ordinary workflow already does, whether the proposed mechanism reduces to a standard method, input availability, an assessable output, the complete-cost comparison, and the cheapest disqualifying observation or analytic argument. Researchers may author analytic qualification inputs; those do not establish production value. Missing code or a paper not reporting a feature does not prove novelty or impossibility.

Select at most one only if (a) its operation is concretely assessable, (b) a finite analytic/source-level question distinguishes a specific proposed mechanism from the strongest ordinary method, and (c) there is an input route not dependent on inventing physical semantics or unreleased assets. Selection may authorize preparation of that bounded qualification, not numerical development this turn. A generic loss, ordinary projection/transfer/quadrature, a new interface or combining named components is insufficient without a concrete capability or full-cost difference to test.

If none survives, report no supported core hypothesis at present and the specific missing information/resource/author decision. Do not automatically generate another batch, reopen protection-cache or D03 experiments, or call scientific uncertainty a passed contribution. Root writes the consolidated decision and updates state; agents own disjoint candidate memos. Zero GPU work is an intended research allocation, not a performance measurement.
