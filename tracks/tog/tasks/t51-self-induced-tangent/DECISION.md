# T51 — consequence of the exact self-closure test

**The current single-carrier family is not exactly closed under its own inviscid flow.** [The complete tangent argument](TANGENT.md) permits arbitrary center, covector and symmetric-covariance derivatives. Parity leaves only translation available for the even nonlinear target; matching it would require one constant translation coefficient to equal a strictly varying radial function. The contradiction is on physical vorticity, not on a gauge potential or a restricted ordinary update.

This closes a concrete uncertainty left by T49: prescribed-affine Gaussian closure does not extend to exact self-consistent closure, even in this one isotropic instance. It also gives an explicit physical residual for the usual center-affine update, including where symmetry makes that residual vanish. These are analytical findings, not a measured instability or a numerical convergence result.

## Effect on the research direction

Retain the ordinary reconstruction and affine/heat identities as reusable mathematical references. Remove any prospective claim that merely coupling those exact affine identities to the reconstructed center velocity/Jacobian yields an exact finite self-consistent fluid model. No such claim was established by the historical tasks, and their original outcomes remain unchanged.

The representation may still be useful as an approximation. A substantive method argument would need a defined physical error criterion, a justified approximation or state-management operation, and a fair comparison with ordinary projection/refitting and other capable representations. Exact membership is not a prerequisite for a convergent particle method, and nonmembership is not a novel mechanism or proof of a practical bottleneck. The present result supplies neither an error tolerance nor the usefulness of any repair.

No Gaussian enrichment, adaptive splitting, gauge variant, best-fit tangent, multi-carrier scene or numerical benchmark is selected automatically from this negative exact-closure result. Selecting a further method task requires a concrete approximation question and decision payoff beyond repeating that a finite ansatz is not exact. No next computational run is selected in T51.
