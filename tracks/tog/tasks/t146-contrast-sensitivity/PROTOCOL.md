# How much does the image choice depend on the registered 16-level threshold?

This retrospective analysis uses only two already measured frame-270 image data sets. The T140 native woodpile scores at 16 and T144's Bonsai target maxima are known. Include every positive display contrast cutoff representable by the registered 2 by 2 average, from 0.25 through 255 in increments of 0.25. Keep the original eligible opacity, camera, light-first preference and 25% required coverage. Analyze both native woodpile views and Bonsai camera 0. Keep the Bonsai second camera undefined. Use no new rendering, different shell, or tuning of the primary evidence.

For each brush and camera, sum the opacity histogram at or above each cutoff, divide by total target opacity, and give the contiguous intervals returning light, full or neither. Report changes and constant regions, including views where both miss throughout. Reconstruct the published 16-level result, check monotonicity per branch, and present an explicit sensitivity curve from these histograms. The output measures sensitivity of the specified image operation. It does not infer a viewer's perceptual threshold or a participant's chosen shot.
