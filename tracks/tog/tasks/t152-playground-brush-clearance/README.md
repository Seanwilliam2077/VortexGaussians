# The fixed Playground brush leaves eight possible gas-seed sites

The three original labels and mapping files occupied a single 575,424-byte contiguous range in the public 1,049,696,735-byte Playground ZIP. All three member names, lengths and CRCs matched the archived central directory. The released full-scene mask selects 80,023 of 1,268,409 fitted Gaussians. The previously acquired original foreground contains exactly those 80,023 ordered cropped coordinates under FieryGS's upstream export. The maximum coordinate overshoot relative to the supplied mapping was 0.00000314 source voxels, within the native 0.001 tolerance. The mapping is specific to Playground. Local Firewoods files with the same names have a different archive identity. The official 256³ material array contains 35,409 wood-label cells. The prescribed Fire-X voxelization, one binary closing and hole filling yield **2,659 wood cells** on the 88 × 88 × 104 simulation grid. The wood center nearest the frozen camera target is unique at index (40,43,17) and world coordinates (−0.08,0.24,0.05). Its 4.4 cm brush changes three wood voxels. The native ignition sphere therefore paints a real, connected selection. The known fitted foreground supplies 35,478 points inside the native collision box and safely inside its opacity/scale thresholds; including them identifies 2,741 guaranteed solid cells. This step used a conservative margin in logit/log-scale space. **Full registration is incomplete.** Eight immediate face neighbors of that footprint remain outside the known solid subset. Each neighbor that is actually open would be seeded at 620 K in one arm and 750 K in the other, because the original seed kernel clips adjacent hot wood at 750 K. Extra non-burnable fitted Gaussians can occupy those cells, and the fitted ground is another obstacle. The reduced mask therefore gives a lower bound of zero on the distance to an open face and an upper bound of eight on mismatched seed cells. There is no measured native distance, gas-sharing interval, image result or timing advantage for this brush. The missing member is `Playground/point_cloud/iteration_30000/scene_point_cloud.ply` in the same ZIP, local-header offset 706,985,269, DEFLATE length 282,741,922, decoded length 314,566,964 bytes and CRC32 `294a82e5`. This member was identified from the previously archived upstream central directory. A complete prepared Playground material/geometry or this full model was absent from the project inventory. Acquiring it lies beyond the single-range T152 intake. The original scene implementation loads this complete fit for opacity, scale, the fitted floor, and native collision support. T152 thus stops at eight identifiable cells, preserving the brush for a full-mask check.
