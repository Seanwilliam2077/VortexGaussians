# Transfer screen on the released FieryGS grid solver

The question is whether exact material threshold queries require a coupled gas trajectory on a separate published solver. Use the pinned FieryGS implementation f70fbe26d22431c7acd93a745861dd47ba4307d7, its Firewoods configuration, and independently attained 128³ checkpoints already archived in the original project. Inspect the wood-temperature, occupancy, coefficient and step operations before adding a candidate algorithm. Check whether the wood/occupancy subsystem reads velocity, pressure, gas temperature or gas density, and whether the gas reads material. Audit the finite archived occupancy/temperature fields at original steps 0, 3, 10, 30 and 60, including occupancy support, number of materials above ignition threshold and connected-component sizes under the released face-neighbor stencil. Carry any mismatches or unavailable fields.
 
If material evolution is autonomous, the strong ordinary baseline may skip all gas for a disposable material query at every frame. A preview of the rendered gas and a request to keep the coupled continuation have different obligations. Do not treat an advantage over full fluid replay as validation of the conditional two-way service proof. If an exact component restriction is possible, count how much a sparse full-material update would already remove before deciding whether dynamic timing could distinguish the alternatives. This screen can qualify or rule out using this particular solver as a second demonstration. It includes no solver installation, black-box approximation, borrowed timing, participant or claim to a new fire simulation.
