## A conservative substep test

Each ordinary gas substep uses the current scene emitters and the coupling grid generated by the preceding cloth update. Cloth then advances in the new gas and splats its next grid. Every participating vertex can contribute to this fixed-point grid. Its 13 channels include area weighted by normal, velocity, momentum, fuel, heat, smoke and steam. The woodpile's ordered donor quota has no counterpart in this path. [Substep schedule](https://github.com/gjnail/blackbody/blob/e2ac951dbfabc6060073943e4c344439ac5f4555/blackbody/engine/engine.py#L350-L409) · [Atomic grid construction](https://github.com/gjnail/blackbody/blob/e2ac951dbfabc6060073943e4c344439ac5f4555/blackbody/engine/wgsl/cloth_splat.wgsl#L40-L94) · [Coarse/fine scalar and velocity inputs](https://github.com/gjnail/blackbody/blob/e2ac951dbfabc6060073943e4c344439ac5f4555/blackbody/engine/cloth.py#L1115-L1138)

A sufficient guard starts with equal complete gas state and matching clocks, grid layouts, solver options and **all other effective gas-side inputs**. It checks equal native feedback flags and the entire effective integer coupling grid for the two cloth states before each gas advance. With a reproducible native step, identical inputs permit one gas evolution; each cloth still advances from its own material and mechanical state. At the first failed check, give each branch the last shared gas state and its own pending feedback, and resume ordinary evolution. Charge both cloth updates, any speculative second branch, grid comparison, synchronization, storage and branch restoration. A retrospective digest of two complete simulations is an oracle for validation; it supplies no editing speedup.

Record three separate boundaries: the edit becoming active, first unequal evolving cloth state and first failed gas-sharing check. Only shared gas updates after actual material-state divergence test the proposed mechanism. Equal gas for a lagged single substep may follow trivially from the update order. If no additional interval survives, retain that result. For a kept edit, verify at least the complete next native update and the requested image against ordinary replay. A checkpoint omitting the previous cloth feedback grid or its filled flag can give the wrong first resumed update. [Checkpoint and render limitations](../t128-external-render-path/README.md)
