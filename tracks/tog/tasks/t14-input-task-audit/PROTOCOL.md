Prepared 27 October 2026, before this task's source inspection. Implements the [decision after T13](../t13-ordinary-feasibility-recovery/NEXT_DECISION.md); no new numerical experiment is authorized by this protocol. T12/T13 method development stays closed.

Question: do either of the two existing primary-source families supply an inspectable, reproducible input and a concrete continued-simulation editing decision for which compatible reconstruction states could matter? This is an input/task audit, not a fresh broad novelty search or proof of a method contribution.

Families are fixed: (1) Lian et al., *Uncertainty-Aware Physical Simulation of Neural Radiance Fields for Fluids*, CMES 2024, starting at the publisher full text; (2) Liu et al., *WildSmoke*, starting at the original arXiv paper and its author-linked project/code. Follow only their direct implementation/data/supplement links and focused searches for those exact families. Do not add a third family.

Budget: two inspection passes per family. Pass 1 identifies documented operations and primary paper/project/code/data links; pass 2 resolves specific missing fields by reading the linked implementation, release inventory or supplement. A pass may contain batched tool calls; log the visited pages and what gap each second-pass read resolves. Stop after the second pass with remaining facts marked unknown. No training, simulation, large third-party downloads, dependency installation, outreach or asset redistribution. Small source files may be read locally outside Git if necessary; only original analysis, URLs, provenance metadata and compact measurements go into this repository.

Record published delivery and operation; ordinary existing editor capabilities; observed data, recoverable states, units, timing and boundary conditions; input accessibility and license; whether multiple independently obtained states share an inspectable observation contract and intervention; and the editing decision and evaluation evidence. Distinguish author demonstrations, researcher-authored possibilities and actual creator requirements. Do not invent a creator, a tolerance, a practical risk threshold or an asset permission. A retrieval failure means not verified through that route, not that the resource does not exist.

Decision: prepare a numerical task only if one family supports a concrete continued-simulation operation, inspectable/reproducible input, and a credible assessment of an editing decision beyond a nonzero difference. Multiple models or pretty editing examples alone do not satisfy that conjunction. If neither qualifies, record **no qualified input/task for further computation** and close D03's current exploration branch pending new evidence. This does not prove the general R19-B hypothesis impossible or close all technical research. No new optimizer/toy is the fallback.

Pre-result clarification during protocol review: two already-downloadable checkpoints are not a prerequisite. Accessible observations and a reproducible ordinary multi-initialization recipe could support preparing a later bounded test, if the common intervention and concrete decision are also specified. Separately record checkpoint-ready, recipe-only and paper-only evidence. Preparation is not a claim that multiple qualified states or useful response differences already exist.

Deliverables: one short source-linked memo per family, a shared qualification table and explicit go/no-go conclusion, a bounded access/cost record, and an independent reasoning/evidence review. Root owns the consolidated README, state updates and commit; source-family agents own separate memos. No one edits old task evidence.
