It still needs a fuller comparison with existing error bounds, compositing-error analyses and Gaussian volume methods before any priority claim. Two of these three families lack acquired full text. Even a correct and informative bound would not establish a useful speed/accuracy tradeoff, a scene-level method or a TOG contribution.

This source screen is closed at the fixed budget. The present task proceeds only to its already fixed qualification. Any later literature acquisition requires a separately stated finite purpose and must preserve these access failures.
