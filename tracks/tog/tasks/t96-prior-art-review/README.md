# Prior art and technical assessment of selective simulation queries

25 November 2026. This is an internal technical review for the current manuscript. I implemented and checked the held-out trunk reference, but did not implement the T91 or T93 methods. I knew the preceding Firewoods results and the T95 proposal. I used a targeted primary-source search for local simulation editing, influence certificates, deferred computation and selective particle-field queries. This comparison identifies concrete overlap. It does not establish priority or supply an external peer review.

## What the closest methods already do

| Prior work and primary source | Established mechanism | Bearing on the present claims |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hammer, Phang, Hicks and Foster, PLDI 2014. [Adapton](https://matthewhammer.org/adapton/) | Recomputes dependencies on demand after inputs change and reuses prior work represented by a demanded computation graph. | Responding to an observer's query, deferring a field evaluation and reusing it at a later demand have prior art. T94 needs an identifiable simulation-specific dependency, a correctness argument under compaction, and a measurable advantage over evaluating the same targets once. |
| Boateng and Krasny, Journal of Computational Chemistry 2013. [Treecodes with disjoint targets and sources](https://sites.lsa.umich.edu/krasny/wp-content/uploads/sites/1435/2025/06/paper_jcc_2013_preprint.pdf) | Builds tree approximations for particle potentials at specified targets using a distinct set of sources. The evaluated treecode itself approximates direct potential evaluation. | It is ordinary to retain all sources and evaluate the field only at selected target sites. The graphics question here is whether the target set can be recovered from post-advance survival, ordered hot-particle admission and requested material output. Our reference is equality to the native tree solver's events, with the same tree approximation. It does not establish exact continuum velocities. |
| Mirtich, SIGGRAPH 2000. [Timewarp Rigid Body Simulation](https://www.merl.com/publications/TR2000-17) | Exploits sparse interaction and speculative local progress with rollback and contact groups to reduce unnecessary synchronization. | Selective advancement and deferring work until interaction are established in graphics. Mirtich returns a rigid-body simulation. Our benchmark requests a regional Boolean, first discrete frame and one witness, and discards the query branch. Restoring its parent alone does not demonstrate resuming a partially advanced world. |
| Harmon, Vouga, Smith, Tamstorf and Grinspun, SIGGRAPH 2009. [Asynchronous Contact Mechanics, §4](https://www.cs.columbia.edu/cg/pdfs/p102-harmon.pdf) | Maintains conservative separation certificates with scheduled failure times. Changes to participating velocities can require reconsidering the certificates. | Conservative bounds and scheduling work only once it may matter already appear in physically based animation. A future-material coupling certificate has to identify a different invariant and show that checking it costs less than the omitted computations. |
| Chen, Levin and Langlois, Computer Graphics Forum 2026. [Fluid Control with Localized Spacetime Windows, §4.2.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.15189v2) | Restricts control optimization to a spacetime region and penalizes differences between buffer particles and their original trajectories. | This is a direct precedent for localized fluid editing. It solves for controls that produce edited motion. T95's prospective promise concerns exact event outputs of a fixed discrete trajectory under explicit service and scheduling assumptions. The soft buffer would need to solve a different problem to be a label oracle. |

The cited methods operate on different physical models. Their reported runtimes cannot substitute for matched timings on this selected-input problem. The absence of this exact brush question in the five papers cannot by itself establish a new algorithm.

## How to discriminate the proposed mechanisms

1. **T93 and T94.** On a disposable step, all sources can still affect each requested velocity through the native tree. A target whose field is skipped also changes compacted rank if it may die. Measure ordinary all-target, rank-only and geometry-culling paths with the same native field arithmetic, same isolation, same region index and actual result delivery. Compare T94 to a simple early positive exit with residual heat tests and to eager exact replay when a negative step must continue. Count fields evaluated before the first answer, deferred fields needed on resumption, duplicate work and forks. A mechanism with no resumed step cannot support a claim about exact continuation. An induction or an audit of resumed states should cover layer order, particle deletion, heat stores, RNG and donor service. Equal final Booleans alone can conceal a broken continuation.
2. **T95.** The potential invariant concerns the next coupling interface. A surviving serviced graph prefix and a finite sequence of emitter IDs known from the current cursor would allow off-region material to evolve without changing emissions, while regional heat still receives all native contributions. A rank-changing death, cursor wrap into newly ignited sites, feedback from ambient heat or fuel-dependent sampling can violate it. Confirm both the positive and failure branches on pre-registered inputs. Inspect whether the same RNG draws, admissions, particles and regional heat occur over every qualified prefix. Keep all fallback costs. An output comparison can then establish exactness on the measured workload; a proof states which other operators it covers.
3. **Strong ordinary comparisons.** Use full exact replay with a precomputed region membership mask, a true first-event early exit and terminal-only regional scan. Compare with an ordinary cache of static donor-neighbor pairs and weights, including cold-build time, bytes and realistic amortization. The donor positions and graph kernel here are fixed; recomputing the identical neighbor query is an avoidable cost. Include the best applicable control instead of selecting the easiest denominator. For the coupling-specific claim, use identical gas treatment and terminal method on both sides, changing only the handling of intermediate material updates.
4. **Falsifiers.** One incorrect issued Boolean, first event frame or witness invalidates an exact query on that input. If decisions settle independently of the proposed processing, bulk timing improvement cannot establish that mechanism. If the ordinary rank test or the static graph cache matches inclusive latency, report what it already solves. If effects require an artificially old frontier, one favorable brush location or an editor who wants only a terminal Boolean, narrow the claim accordingly. Two point orders on one asset share much more state than two independent studies.

## Evidence available before the follow-up timings

The trunk's 18 nonempty shell queries were selected before its simulation. The T91 and T93 operators were frozen before release of that asset's outcomes. An independent verifier matched all 1,008 T90 timed and warmup answers to the registered point IDs and first frames. The distinction between completing the requested final step and stopping earlier materially changes the interpretation of T93. In eight queries native replay stopped before the requested terminal frame. T93's median paired delivered cost was 0.995 times the native region baseline there. In ten queries the requested final frame was reached; its ratio was 0.458 versus native and 0.732 versus rank-only. This supports additional savings on the targeted discrete stage beyond the rank-only ablation within that subgroup. It does not show saving intermediate complete steps. [T92 evidence](../t92-transfer-workload/README.md) · [T90 comparison](../t90-callable-regional-query/evidence/dead-tree/SCORE.json)

The geometries currently comprise a trained woodpile foreground and one external CC0 trunk mesh converted to splats, with distinct seeds and investigator-authored edits. They increase confidence in native correctness and in the restricted case. Both use the same deterministic layered solver, positive heat, append-only surviving frontier regime and highly ordered processing budgets. There is no observation of an editor using this query to revise a shot. A transferable systems contribution needs demonstrated causal skipping beyond generic lazy evaluation and culling, enough diverse workloads to expose when it fails, and a comparison with the best ordinary way to deliver the requested output.
