# T59 — same physical reference, ordinary direct elliptic binding

The [T58 decision](../t58-separable-solver-qualification/DECISION.md) selects one coupled solver-port qualification, not a new method or changed equation. The source imports unchanged [T56](../t56-coupled-reference/reference.py), which imports unchanged [T55 reconstruction](../t55-axisymmetric-reference/reference.py). Only the in-memory Poisson function binding changes to a checked adapter around the unchanged [T58 solver](../t58-separable-solver-qualification/solver.py). No historical source file is edited.

The physical system remains free-space inviscid axisymmetric no-swirl Euler, q=omega_theta/r, advected by ur=-r*phi_z and uz=2*phi+r*phi_r, with -(phi_rr+3*phi_r/r+phi_zz)=q. Initial cell averages come from q0=1.5*exp(-(r²+z²)/2). The annular conservative reconstruction, minmod joint positivity limiter, upwind shared face fluxes, zero-exterior inflow, SSPRK2, quadrature and current-q Green boundary construction retain their original definitions in [T56 FORMULATION](../t56-coupled-reference/FORMULATION.md). Both time stages recompute their own current-q velocity.

The elliptic FV system is exactly the T58-qualified operator. Let W_i=(r_(i+1)^4-r_i^4)/4, L_i=r_i^3/(W_i*h), U_i=r_(i+1)^3/(W_i*h). Its radial diagonal is L_i+U_i, with extra U_last at the outer row; radial neighbors are -L_i/-U_i. Axial diagonal is 2/h², with another 1/h² at both endpoints, neighbors -1/h². The original RHS adds 2*U_last*gR, 2*gminus/h², 2*gplus/h². The adapter independently assembles this operator and RHS and checks sqrt(sum(W*(Aphi-b)²)/sum(W*b²))<=1e-10 after every direct solve. The original residual is evaluated without using FFT, modes or factors. Positive finite pivots and the exceptional sine mode remain checked in T58. Factors are rebuilt each call.

All per-stage boundary, elliptic, residual, reconstruction and transport costs count. All six cases construct their own ordinary dense Green matrices, including the two port-regression cases. S64/S128 old fields are only comparison data. The new case wrapper preserves the original mathematical update and adds snapshot persistence, explicit K lifetime and direct-method accounting. It saves a snapshot NPZ completely before its JSON completion/hash record, then saves full case arrays after final K-dependent diagnostics and K release. Partial files without completed valid metadata are not usable checkpoints or completed trajectories.

Field errors use the original common core r in [0,4], z in [-4,4], original 3x2 Gauss evaluation on the finer partition and no alignment. The q-represented physical vorticity norm has weight 2*pi*r³ and velocity weight 2*pi*r. Physical comparisons retain their second/control/finer denominator. Port regression calls compare(new,old), keeping old-field denominators. Full-box circulation-weighted axial centroid and covariance use the original diagnostics; q-squared invariant and axial impulse retain 2*pi*integral(r*q²) and pi*integral(r³*q). No core-restricted moments are introduced.

The entire task remains an empirical short-window reference qualification, without a formal discretization/error certificate, complete t=1 result, Gaussian candidate comparison or TOG mechanism.
