# Can two different native feedback grids have the same effect?

Registered before viewing the frame-1 and frame-2 word arrays. This is a secondary, offline analysis of the two ordinary checkpoints already requested by T131. The two times belong to one unheated trajectory. Treat their buffers as alternative inputs to a single gas hook with common clocks, finite gas state, material-independent forcing, enabled hooks, layout, and native formats. These are counterfactual conditions for comparing the maps. The two physical simulations at different times are not claimed interchangeable. No camera, edit or additional device invocation is involved.

Compare full grid equality with this sufficient operator test for the fixed 13-channel native implementation:

* Require equal fuel, heat, smoke and steam words, channels 6–8 and 12, and equal momentum deposits on each axis, channels 9–11. Conservatively check all of them even where a particular enabled kernel would not use them.
* Require nonnegative area words in both grids and matching positive-area supports per axis. For each cell with a nonzero momentum deposit, require identical area and area-weighted velocity words on that axis throughout its one-cell Chebyshev neighborhood. This includes every pair of cells that can appear in the same eight-corner interpolation. At other cells, differences in the magnitudes of those two fields cannot change a velocity increment: on any intervening zero-impulse stencil both branches follow the same area-presence branch and add the same zero. Requiring equal positive supports also preserves the distinction between skipping an addition and adding zero to a signed zero. Applies on this 2:1 aligned grid, where nonzero weights do not underflow. Inequality is inconclusive, never an assertion that the gas differs.

Report per-channel changed/nonzero counts, distinct area supports, the number of offending words near live impulses, and the complete pass/fail decision regardless of sign. Report deterministic level-6 zlib bytes for both raw complete grids and their history-dependent four-channel tails using identical little-endian word order and codec. These are payload bytes, not the whole checkpoint, installation cost or a speed estimate. Exact recurrence can require additional clocks, flags and other native state. Do not choose a different pair, invent an edit, or equate a pass here with a useful shared post-divergence interval.
