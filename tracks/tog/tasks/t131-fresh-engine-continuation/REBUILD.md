# Can the ordinary cloth solver reconstruct its pending feedback?

Source audit of Blackbody `e2ac951dbfabc6060073943e4c344439ac5f4555`. A second native splat is a fair restoration control. Its ability to reproduce all 13 feedback words per cell depends on what was accumulated during the last cloth update. This note reports source behavior; the numerical cold-restore comparison belongs in the experiment receipt.

| Coupling channels | Inputs to the original splat | What a cold restore can recover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5, area and area-weighted velocity | Position `X.xyz` maps each vertex to eight coarse cells. Normal and area come from `N`, velocity from `V.xyz`, and `S.y` marks consumed vertices. | Native full cloth checkpoints save these fields in float32. With identical vertices, grid coordinates and arithmetic they reproduce the deposited words. |
| 6–8, fuel, heat and smoke | The burning flag `S.z`, fabric index `V.w`, material table `M`, fuel-per-kg constant, and the original step's ambient and flame temperatures. | Regenerate the same material table from the same fabric specifications and use `scene.look(checkpoint_frame)`. The material table includes density, burn time, fuel, smoke, burn temperature and flammability. |
| 9–12, momentum impulse and steam rate | The predict step accumulates `IMP.xyz`, and evaporation accumulates `IMP.w`. Steam uses the inverse elapsed water-update time. Each vertex's splat reads and zeroes all four values; the host resets the elapsed accumulator. | The post-splat checkpoint omits these consumed inputs. It stores `MP` (force from matter), `P` (including wetness) and `W` (water state); none records the consumed drag impulse or boiled mass. A re-splat with empty accumulators reproduces these four channels only when the original aggregated integer words are also zero. |

Fixed-point integer additions and a heat maximum combine the per-vertex contributions on the GPU. With identical per-vertex quantization, integer reduction order does not affect the result. Neither a positive temperature nor zero burning establishes the four historical channels. **Cold control.** At a saved post-splat boundary, restore into a fresh engine and check that the original coupling input was ready and had the same origin, cell size and dimensions. Call `cloth.prepare_frame(solver)`, then `cloth.splat(batch, solver, scene.look(checkpoint_frame))` inside a submitted native batch. Read the **entire** grid and flags before advancing. Compare channel-by-channel and verify the uninterrupted successor. Charge the two GPU passes, any readback/check and restored ancillary state. A four-channel sidecar plus re-splat would retain 16,384 raw grid bytes versus 53,248 for all 13 words in the fixed neutral layout. Real payload size must also account for compressing the sparse ordinary full grid, metadata, I/O and reconstruction. The source result alone establishes neither a byte nor a latency advantage for encoded checkpoints.

**Boundaries.** Preserve a pending-disabled flag at boundaries where no effective splat occurred. Domain growth can follow the last splat and precede checkpoint creation. In that case the checkpoint solver layout may differ from the original deposition layout. The per-frame preparation sets the solver steam flag and allocates the coarse grid from the solver dimensions. The fine fire path reads the same coupling grid but also uses its own fine fields and wetness flags. The wetness latch during dynamics can remain true after water drains, whereas loading infers it from the surviving vertex water. Wet, growing or high-resolution continuations therefore require independent flag, layout and field checks.
