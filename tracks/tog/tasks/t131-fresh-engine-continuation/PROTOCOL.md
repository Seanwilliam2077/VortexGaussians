# Clean-process cloth and gas continuation at a known checkpoint

Registered 27 November 2026 before requesting a device. Use precisely T130's default unheated fabric and no-emitter scene with 32 cells on its longest axis, 24 fps, no preroll, no fine grid, identical final settings, pinned Blackbody source, isolated runtime and NVIDIA D3D12 adapter. Do not render, show artist candidates, or use an artist brief.

Use three serial, fresh Python processes/devices. The reference saves the native frame-1 disk checkpoint, reads its complete native gas, cloth and ember checkpoint fields and pending cloth-to-gas integer feedback, then advances uninterrupted through frame 2. Keep the supplemental frame-1 feedback bytes and their dimensions, native cell size/origin, channel count and ready flag. Record raw byte size, content digests, transfer and I/O clocks. Each restore process starts with no evolved engine or buffered feedback and attaches the identical disk cache read-only. Require a real native disk hit at frame 1 without a simulated step. Capture the native pre-step state. One process continues unmodified, one applies the predeclared supplement before continuing. No candidate, camera or new physics are used.

For the supplement, validate the scene signature, parent frame, currently restored gas grid, cell size, origin, native channel count and saved feedback array dimensions and digest. Use the upstream coupling buffer allocator for those dimensions, copy the saved words into it, then restore the ready flag. Compare against the reference immediately before the step as well as after frame 2. Every recorded native field, layout, ready flag and array digest must match to claim agreement on this fixture. Also report separately whether upstream restore loses a pending input, whether the observed successor differs, and whether supplying the missing input changes the observed successor. The endpoint can agree even when its starting input differs. Another omitted field or a cold-start exception closes that arm as an incomplete or failed qualification.

Reserve one 180-second worker per arm, inclusive of import, native setup, diagnostic transfers and cleanup. Native baseline failure does not cancel the separately registered supplement arm if the shared artifact is available and validated; failure of the reference ends the experiment. Do not add a fourth arm, introduce heat, or silently fix or modify the upstream implementation. Preserve errors/partial receipts and all actual wall times. Use separate operator and verification commands, and require exclusive device usage. These are single diagnostic runs on one unheated fixture. Clocks include synchronization; they do not measure a speed advantage, cross-edit window, final playback or an artist choice.
