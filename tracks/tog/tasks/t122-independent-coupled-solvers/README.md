# T122 · Independent graphics solvers with two-way combustible material

**Use Blackbody's existing Burning curtain shot for the first outside observation.** Its cotton feeds a grid gas solver, the gas can ignite the cotton, and the released renderer produces a camera-space flame-emission pass. The preset is procedural, with its own authored lighter, sheet and camera. This makes it possible to study a finished-shot quantity without obtaining another reconstruction. This was a source inspection of four primary projects. No solver, asset, simulation or benchmark was installed or run. PhysStudio's editable ignition region heats occupied wood and can seed neighboring air from the resulting temperatures *before* dynamic transport. A broader nonempty hot footprint may therefore change gas at initialization. Even when initial gas matches, wood exchanges heat immediately, the solver uses grid pressure projection, and parallel floating additions may vary between repeats. A surface match, matching wood IDs or a bounded material stencil would not certify identical gas, final flame or bitwise continuation. The paper that inspired a component and this open implementation must be cited separately. ### Published context

Flamescapes (SIGGRAPH Asia 2026) explicitly describes solid-to-gas fuel production and gas/solid thermal feedback via convection, conduction and radiation. FlameForge (SCA 2025) provides a volumetric wood-combustion model and demonstrates coupling to deformable structures. Their official project pages provide papers and visualizations; neither inspected page provided a runnable solver/sample pair. This qualifies the context only. No assertion about unreleased implementations or scientific priority follows from those pages. ### First experiment and falsifier

Use the existing Blackbody camera, cotton, solver and lighter. Contrast the original lighter footprint with one smaller footprint at the same place, times and fuel/temperature settings. Measure **rendered flame emission in frame space**, paired with the native beauty image, as well as material ignition and gas divergence at the work boundary. Reducing an active gas emitter can change gas on its very first affected source pass. Ordinary caching before the edited frame and reuse of an already identical rendered frame are the first controls. A positive equal prefix entirely before the lighter activates earns zero credit toward a new solver mechanism. Charge any view generation, readback and checkpoint necessary for an actual editing decision; test repeatability before interpreting exact equality. A nontrivial common dependency after the changed emission begins remains an open question. See the [follow-on protocol](../t124-external-curtain-observation/PROTOCOL.md).

The local bundled Python is 3.12.14 and reports NumPy but no Blackbody, wgpu, PySide6 or OpenEXR package. Windows lists AMD Radeon 890M and NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5070 Laptop graphics; native adapter admission, shader compilation, procedural shot load and full state/renderer readback have yet to be established. Source-level coupling and two native renderers give a falsifiable journal direction. They add no independent numerical transfer result or reviewer-score improvement.
