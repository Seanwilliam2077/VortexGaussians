# T74 limited primary-source context

Read 2026-11-01. This is a bounded context check, not a novelty review or a claim that unlocated work does not exist. Five search queries targeted this mechanism/context; three primary-source targets were opened, including repeated access to the two arXiv pages. No code, implementation or benchmark was acquired. The local T52 and T67–T73 evidence supplies the actual mathematical starting point.

- [Dynamic Divergence-Free Kernels, arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.17693v1): the abstract and portions of the HTML background/formulation were accessible. The authors represent velocity with matrix-valued divergence-free kernels and solve for time-varying parameters by optimization. The displayed construction applies the divergence-free differential operator to a compact radial basis. The authors acknowledge computational overhead relative to traditional first-order approaches. Thus an analytically divergence-free kernel plus parameter evolution alone is not a sufficient new claim here. This is not a full-paper/code reproduction; its velocity representation is not the transported scalar q used in T74.
- [Fluid Simulation on Vortex Particle Flow Maps](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.21946): the primary abstract describes particles carrying vorticity and flow maps, a grid-based velocity reconstruction, higher-order map information and boundary treatment. Its metadata identifies the SIGGRAPH 2025 TOG paper. This already places transported vorticity and flow maps within an established method family. Only the abstract/metadata were read; no quantitative comparison or assertion about its exact invariant preservation follows.
- [A multi-moment vortex method for 2D viscous fluids](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0021999111006437): a search result mentioned Hermite expansions and deforming Gaussian vortex approaches, but opening the publisher page returned403. This remains an access-limited lead, not verified full text and not a basis for declaring the T74 calculation novel or previously proved.

The T74 positive-kernel energy argument is independently derived in this task. Its correctness is separately checkable; its originality has not been established. Even if new as a lemma, a limitation of one narrow ansatz would not by itself demonstrate a useful graphics method or a TOG core contribution.
