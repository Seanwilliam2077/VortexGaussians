research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:94: ## Contribution and remaining gaps
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:95: 
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:96: No new core method or compelling system-level advantage has yet been
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:97: established. Existing work already covers several tempting broad claims:
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:98: 
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:99: - [DDFKs](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.17693v1) provides forward fluid simulation
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:100:   with divergence-free kernels and vorticity-based updates;
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:101:   [Vortex Particle Flow Maps](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.21946v1) transports
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:102:   vorticity with flow-map derivatives. Gaussian deformation and Cauchy stretching
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:103:   alone do not identify a new solver.
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:104: - [GI 2022 surfel/VPM boundaries](https://graphicsinterface.org/wp-content/uploads/gi2022-10.pdf)
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:105:   and [VEMPIC](https://www.dgp.toronto.edu/projects/vempic/) are relevant strong
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:106:   alternatives for boundary handling. A point-based representation does not by
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:107:   itself resolve missing geometry or make a physical boundary uniquely known.
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:108: - [HyFluid](https://github.com/y-zheng18/HyFluid) already optimizes vortex
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:109:   particles from video; [LagrangianSplats](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.09299v1)
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:110:   uses divergence-free transport of Gaussian appearance. High-frame-rate RGB
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:111:   alone does not generally identify physical temperature or a unique velocity.
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:112: 
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:113: These comparisons reject the currently specified candidates, not every possible
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:114: research direction. A system contribution need not invent every component, but
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:115: must demonstrate a substantive new capability or quality–cost advantage under
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:116: fair comparison. Current evidence does not establish that either.
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:117: 
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:118: The next implementation must be motivated by a concrete technical hypothesis
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:119: and a baseline capable of using the same information. Do not extend the proxy
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:120: resolution sweep, rename standard moment/remapping methods, or start expensive
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:121: inverse training merely to produce another phase. Full reaction/lifecycle
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:122: accounting, physical boundaries, real asset replay and matched-quality
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:123: whole-system evaluation remain missing from the research path. Adding these
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:124: features would improve engineering completeness; it would not automatically
research/EVIDENCE_STATUS.md:125: establish a research contribution.
