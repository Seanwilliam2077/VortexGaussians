## Next finite action: T14 input and task qualification audit

Use the two closest leads already identified in the [bounded prior review](../../COUNTERFACTUAL_PRIOR_SCREEN.md): uncertainty-aware fluid reconstruction and WildSmoke's reconstruction-to-wind editing. This is an audit of available evidence and executable input, not another general novelty search. Their publication describes relevant operations; it does not imply that public code, trained data, an ensemble suitable for continuation, or creator-validated requirements are available.

Budget: at most two primary paper/project/code families, at most two inspection passes per family, zero GPU simulation or training, no third-party large asset downloads, no outreach, and no changes to existing experiments. First inspect their documented input/output and released example paths, if any; then resolve only concrete gaps from that inspection. Stop if neither supplies the necessary evidence rather than broadening the search indefinitely.

The deliverable is one concise, source-linked qualification table and a go/no-go decision. For each family, record:

1. The actual delivered artifact and editing operation demonstrated by the authors, with a precise source location. Distinguish published author demonstration from a newly invented researcher task and from a human creator's stated need.
2. Whether the operation requires continued dynamics and re-rendering, and what an ordinary image/volume deformation or the authors' own editor already delivers. Do not demand 3D merely because the current model is 3D.
3. Which observations, initial/state variables, units, time interval, boundary conditions and editor parameters can actually be recovered from accessible evidence. Record asset access and license limits without downloading large files.
4. Whether at least two independently obtained, observation-compatible states and a common intervention can be inspected, or what exact input is missing. An ensemble with different renderers, uncontrolled densities or unmatched histories does not automatically qualify.
5. The concrete editing decision that could change and how it would be assessed. A difference image or nonzero scalar span alone is insufficient. If this decision is researcher-authored, label it accordingly; do not invent creator testimony, tolerances, acceptable risk or perceptual validity.

## Decision rule

Advance to preparing a single numerical task only if one source family supports a concrete continued-simulation operation, supplies inspectable/reproducible input and a credible way to examine an editing decision beyond a mere numerical difference. Before any run, freeze its source-specific observation contract, ordinary baseline, costs and stopping rule as a new task. This does not transfer T12's pixel quantization assumptions to another dataset or claim a new method.

If those prerequisites are absent, report **no qualified input/task for further computation** and close this exploration branch pending new evidence. Repeated synthetic counterexamples or progressively stronger solvers are not the fallback. Continuing technical research remains authorized, but the next hypothesis would require its own concrete operation and prior-work argument rather than inheriting an unearned TOG claim.
