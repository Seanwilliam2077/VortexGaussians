# T72 — a search-resolution hypothesis, not a new method

Saved-state analysis found **11 fully unchanged-geometry updates on the finer path, versus 2 on the coarse path**. During these steps only circulation and mixture weight change. The coarse trajectory first changes its original dominant Gaussian at step11 (t=.6875); the fine trajectory retains it through step29 (t=.90625). The fine path's steps24–29 increase the retained target-residual fraction from .15564 to .22209. These patterns precede or accompany the observed time-step divergence, but do not establish its cause.

The unchanged search source uses finest geometric polls (.025R,.025Z,.05Z), with no explicit h scaling. For a candidate update d, the exact improvement identity is ΔJ=2h⟨F,d⟩−‖d‖². A fixed geometric displacement can therefore cease to improve a smaller-step target, while continuous weight changes remain available. State-dependent bank motion, small-weight births and coincident-component splitting prevent treating this as a global hard-grid error bound. See the [mechanism analysis](MECHANISM.md) and [17-time/48-update tables](TABLES.md).

There are no selected exact-boundary hits or selected cancellation flags. The only exhausted final poll is at coarse step16, later than the first sensitivity failure. Neither fact proves global search quality or excludes rejected-candidate effects. The [independent review](../../../validation/t72-search-sensitivity-review.md) checks the extraction against saved scalars and reviews the formula and its limits; it does not rerun a numerical method.

These are bookkeeping costs; source reading and writing time are not included. **Zero scientific invocations, integrals, fits, new trajectories or raw-array reads occur.** The tracked scientific-child subtotal remains2017.6732525997795 seconds, not total research cost.
