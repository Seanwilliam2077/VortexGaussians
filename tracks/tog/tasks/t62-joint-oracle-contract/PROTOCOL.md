## Joint gates and parameter selection

Preserve D8=[0,8] x [-8,8], weight 2pi r³, relative physical-vorticity L2 <=.10, absolute axial-centroid error <=.1 and covariance Frobenius error <=.1 times the actual initial S256 reference norm D0. No alignment after scoring, radial-centroid veto, fitted reference, alternative norm or added conservation equality. A>0 and positive finite widths are mandatory. Report model circulation and axial impulse, including their deviations from the reference, without silently making them new rejection gates.

All parameter selection uses S256 alone. With x=gamma sr²=a+rho u, y=sz²=d+rho v and z0=mu_z+.1w, admit u²+v²<=1 and |w|<=1, and recheck the original analytic moment gates on the resulting floating-point parameters. An exactly boundary construction rounded outside a physical gate is ineligible; do not relax the gate. The saved-data analysis gives rho>0 and positive width lower bounds. Free A is eliminated by B/M on the primary quadrature. S128 is sensitivity only: hold **A, sr, sz and z0 all fixed**, with no refit or alternative selection based on S128.

Use this deterministic ordinary search:

1. Evaluate moment-matched geometry u=v=w=0 and its optimal positive primary-rule amplitude. If it meets all three primary gates, select it immediately for the final score confirmation; skip the grid and local searches. A failed final qualification does not trigger a fallback search.
2. Otherwise evaluate the 65 points from u,v,w in {-1,-.5,0,.5,1}, keeping u²+v²<=1. This is 13 disk points times five axial points. Evaluating the already tested center again is allowed and counted. Sort eligible records by squared field error and then lexicographic (u,v,w); take the best three distinct grid coordinates as local starts.
3. For each start, perform at most 30 coordinate-poll cycles. Initial step h=.25 in normalized coordinates. In fixed order +u,-u,+v,-v,+w,-w, evaluate eligible neighbors, completing the poll before changing the current point. Move to the strictly smaller-error best neighbor (lexicographic tie break), retaining h; with no strict improvement halve h. Stop that start when h<=2^-10 or after 30 cycles. Outside-domain neighbors are logged as rejected and not evaluated. Repeated eligible coordinates may be evaluated again, and every evaluation is counted. Do not expand step, restart, change seeds or run another algorithm.
4. Select the minimum-error eligible geometry among all evaluated records, with the same lexicographic tie break. Confirm this one selected candidate even if its field score exceeds .10. No global-optimality claim follows from this search.

The cap is 1+65+3x30x6=606 S256 geometry evaluations, including duplicates; rejected neighbors cost time but not an objective evaluation. Baseline calibration adds exactly two separate contractions, one per reference, at the frozen T60 geometry and fixed A=1.5. No dense-grid coverage or local convergence claim is inferred from exhausting the cap.

## Evaluator and fixed qualification

Construct the 3x2 polynomial rule and 8x6/16x12 Gaussian score rules once each in the same child: six unique one-dimensional orders 3,2,8,6,16,12, totaling 47 nodes. All construction, loading and checks count. Recompute the two endpoint reference moments and C=||qR||² using the unchanged 3x2 rule; compare saved values with relative 3e-11/absolute 3e-14 readback tolerance. This is scalar/array consistency, not a replacement physical gate. Require finite arrays, strictly increasing expected coordinates, correct shapes and grid, positive C/circulation, nonnegative q and reconstruction corners. Corner positivity implies cellwise PL positivity; no hidden clipping.

Use separable **8x6 Gauss contractions** for B and M, as derived in DERIVATION, and C from the polynomial rule. This is the same fixed primary quadrature in different algebraic form, not an approximate new model or novel integration method. It avoids subtracting nearly equal erf-tail primitives. For calibration use A=1.5, sr=sz=1 and z0=c from T54; do not eliminate amplitude at this calibration point. Compute its contraction score against S256 and S128 and recompute the direct 8x6 and 16x12 squared-difference scores. Require each direct score to match its corresponding stored T60 score within 1e-9 absolute, contraction versus direct primary within 1e-9, and original primary/confirmation score agreement <=1e-6. Calibration failure stops before geometry selection; no changed rule or tolerance.

After selection, independently accumulate nonnegative weighted (A f−qR)² using the original direct 8x6 and 16x12 tensor-node loops against both references. The direct 8x6 score remains the reported primary score. Require the selected S256 contraction versus direct primary agreement <=1e-9 and both reference rule differences <=1e-6. Compare the direct denominators with their recomputed polynomial C using relative 3e-11/absolute 3e-14. Both rules use the same frozen candidate parameters; do not use confirmation to refit. Save direct numerators/denominators, D8 model circulation/physical-vorticity norms, analytic full-space totals and differences, moment gates, all raw search records and timings.

The algebra C−B²/M can lose relative accuracy near zero. Do not silently clamp a negative value to zero, reinterpret it as a successful fit, or raise precision adaptively. Nonfinite B/M, nonpositive B or M, negative computed squared residual, inconsistent moments or score mismatch closes the invocation as a qualification failure with partial records. Positive but inaccurate cancellation is checked by the independent direct-square score for the selected candidate. This is empirical numerical qualification, not an interval or global certificate.

Maximum full Gaussian score work is eight rule integrals: calibration and selected candidate, each on S256/S128 with two rules. Their combined upper bound is 78,643,200 node-cell points, reference reconstruction points and Gaussian field points each. Polynomial reference qualification adds 983,040 node-cell points. Search contractions have their own counters for reference cell visits, axis-node exponent evaluations, contractions and rejected neighbors; do not merge axis evaluations with full node-cell points or label either count as FLOPs. All qualification and failed work belongs to the complete cost.

One existing OS observer samples lifetime peak working set every .25 s; stop above **512 MiB**, on a live observer error or at the time cap. Only per-process thread environment limits may be set; no system changes. Exclusive marker and output writes prevent a second invocation. There is no pilot, retry or automatic budget extension.

The budget is a heuristic based on T60's saved 8.7498233-second scoring phase for 196,608,000 full node-cell points and the much smaller O(cells) contractions. It is not a timing guarantee or fair speed comparison. Report full supervised time separately from nested phase attribution; add any scientific failure to cumulative cost. Keep large arrays local with hashes and save partial search/check receipts on failure.
