## Conditions on the consolidated decision

1. **Do not require two downloadable checkpoints in advance.** Public observations plus a reproducible ordinary multi-initialization recipe, common intervention and assessable decision can justify preparing a finite baseline task. Preparation need not presuppose that multiple compatible states will actually be found. Here those linked prerequisites have not all been established.
2. **Separate reproduction gaps from research impossibility.** Missing author code or stored pressure does not imply no valid continuation: a declared new common model may resample velocity/density, recompute a projection, and explicitly set units, sources and boundaries. It must not be called exact author reproduction. Such choices also do not automatically establish a useful editing decision or complete the missing observation/recipe chain.
3. **Do not splice the families.** CMES's ensemble and WildSmoke's wind demonstration cannot jointly count as one qualified ensemble-intervention task. Their observation, state, optical and dynamical contracts have not been connected. A future cross-family construction would be a separately justified researcher-authored task.
4. **A human brief is not the only admissible task basis.** A researcher may define a concrete, motivated operation and an evaluation that distinguishes useful decisions from mere nonzero image/field differences. Such a task must be labelled as researcher-authored and compared with a fair ordinary workflow. The current no-go is not based on a requirement to obtain creator testimony, an intervention ground truth, or an already delivered pair before any research can proceed.
5. **Keep existing capability and cost claims narrow.** WildSmoke already demonstrates reconstruction-to-wind/obstacle continuation. Reimplementing that operation alone is not new capability; its own full continuation pipeline would be a relevant ordinary baseline if the contract were recovered. Neither family inspection measures editing performance or proves new ambiguity-aware benefit. Licenses for articles do not automatically license code/data/checkpoints; unknown redistribution terms are not scientific failure.

These conditions support stopping this D03 input branch pending new evidence. They do not support an impossibility, uniqueness, safety, general-method failure or general technical-research closure claim. No new optimizer, toy or source family is implied as a fallback.
