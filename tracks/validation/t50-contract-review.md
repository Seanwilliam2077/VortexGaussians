2. **Gauge blending inference is correct in its stated continuum scope.** For the linear free-space Helmholtz projector P, set m_r=r m+(1-r)P m. Idempotence gives P m_r=P m, and curl(P m)=curl m gives the same instantaneous vorticity. These are physical-field identities, not merely a small norm residual. They do not establish stability of a finite discretization or equality after future approximate updates, especially with a different boundary projection.

With r<1 the latter survives in m_r with coefficient 1-r; no single constant-covector Gaussian can equal that function. The special r=1 and zero-field cases are not evidence for or against stability. The physical vorticity can remain unchanged even while its chosen potential leaves the parameter class, so this inference does not show physical error, a failed fluid method or a need for a novel repair. The drafts preserve this distinction.
