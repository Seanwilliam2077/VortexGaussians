Which flame looks more like real fire?

You will see pairs of short fire clips, side by side, looping. For each pair, click the one that looks more like real fire — or press F for left, J for right. Let each pair loop at least once before deciding, but answer by overall impression rather than by studying details; there are no right answers.

The session is 18 trials, about 5 minutes. It is anonymous — no personal data is collected and nothing is uploaded; your answers are saved to a file on your own machine at the end, which you then send back to the experimenter. You may stop at any time by closing this page.

Please view in a darkened room if you can, and do not adjust your display brightness during the session.
